A SMALL GUIDE TO BIG IDEAS

Find your flow.

  1. Start with a pulse. Press Play, then toggle steps in any of the four rows. The 16 steps form one repeating bar.
  2. Make it your own. Change the tempo from 60–180 BPM. Click a voice name to hear it, use M to mute it, or adjust its level.
  3. Keep the idea. Export WAV saves one bar plus a short release tail. Save project keeps the editable steps and settings. Open project brings them back.

Keyboard: Space plays/stops when not editing a control. Arrow keys move within the step grid. Enter or Space toggles a focused step. Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes; Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z redoes.

This is an original, deliberately small learning project inspired by pattern-based music workflows. It is not FL Studio, is not affiliated with Image-Line, and does not open FLP files or host plugins. Audio pauses when this tab goes into the background. All sounds are synthesized locally.
